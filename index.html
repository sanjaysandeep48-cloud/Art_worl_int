ABOUT US

Spaces designed with purpose and built with care.

Art Work Interior is a full-service provider of Interior Services and Building Construction, dedicated to delivering high-quality design, construction, and project management solutions.

Established with a vision to transform spaces, we combine creativity, technical expertise, and efficient execution to bring our clients’ visions to life.

We specialize in crafting beautiful, functional interiors and durable, innovative structures for residential, commercial, and institutional clients.

From conceptual design and material selection to construction and finishing, our integrated approach ensures seamless project delivery — on time and within budget.

We prioritize collaboration, transparency, and attention to detail, working closely with clients at every stage to achieve results that reflect their unique style, needs, and aspirations.

PROJECT EXECUTION APPROACH

A structured process for reliable delivery.

Our process is built around review, planning, approval, execution, monitoring, completion and handover.

01

Review

Scrutinize BOQs and drawings; highlight anomalies for clarification.

02

Plan

Estimate schedule and arrange manpower and resources.

03

Approve

Submit samples, seek approvals and generate project drawings.

04

Mobilize

Construct site office and stores and deliver resources to site.

05

Execute

Construct as per design and validate deviations and escalations.

06

Monitor

Monitor design, quality, time and project goals with periodic updates.

07

Complete

Complete the project as agreed and coordinate inspection with the client.

08

Handover

Complete the project handover and provide project documents.
